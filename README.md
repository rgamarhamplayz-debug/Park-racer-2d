# Park-racer-2d
Neon Park Racer: Ascension is an arcade-style driving game built for mobile and desktop browsers. Players navigate a high-speed parkway, dodging procedural traffic while managing a unique "Jump" ability to soar over obstacles.
As you progress, the speed and density of traffic increase dynamically, testing your reflexes to their absolute limit.
Key Features
The Garage (Meta-Progression): Collect gold coins and exchange your survival score for currency. Spend your wealth on permanent upgrades for Engine Speed, Handling, and Jump Battery.
6-Lane Challenge: Unlike standard 3-lane runners, the 6-lane layout offers more complex movement patterns and tighter escapes.
Hardened Controls: Optimized with modern Pointer Events for zero-lag responsiveness on touchscreens and keyboards.
The 10k Secret: Reach a score of 10,000 to unlock Cosmic Drift Mode, transforming the city park into a psychedelic, RGB-fueled space void with custom physics and visuals.
Persistent Saving: Your upgrades and coin totals are saved locally, ensuring your progress is never lost.
